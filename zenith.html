Zenith.

Your kitchen's true north.

HACCP logging that doesn't fight you.

Zenith is Triarc's hygiene, compliance, and daily-operations instrument: HACCP self-control, shift tasks, temperature logs, inspections, staff confirmations, and control-ready exports.

Built for kitchens and bars that need daily operational usefulness and clean evidence at inspection time, not a paper folder that only matters on control day.

How it works

Every check leaves a trail without slowing the kitchen.

Capture

Staff record temperatures, cleaning work, inspections, goods receipt, and training confirmations directly on tablet or phone.

Prove

Missed work, closed days, late completions, responsible staff, and corrective actions stay visible instead of disappearing into paper gaps.

Export

Management can produce clean PDF evidence for control visits and keep daily operations moving from the same dashboard.

What it can cover

  • Temperature logs per fridge, freezer, and fresh-fish device
  • Cleaning and shift task lists with staff confirmation
  • Daily self-control inspection overview
  • Bar, staff, shift-task, shift-log, completion, temperature-log, and inspection entities
  • Goods receipt with temperature, freshness, and traceability records
  • Training module for documented staff instruction
  • PDF export for inspection-ready records
  • Supplier and product catalog with order emails
  • Optional automatic temperature monitoring and alerts
