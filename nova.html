Nova.

Every shift in its orbit.

The schedule that survives the week.

Nova is Triarc's staffing instrument: a mobile-first rota, application, exchange, attendance, and staff-management system for hospitality teams.

Designed for the week as it really happens: open shifts, staff applications, direct swaps, admin approvals, understaffing alerts, and attendance views.

How it works

The rota stays alive after it is published.

Publish

Managers build the week by outlet, role, and day, then keep the current and next week visible for staff and admins.

Exchange

Staff can list shifts, apply for open listings, send direct requests, and route changes through admin approval.

Measure

Attendance views show who worked when, where, in which role, and for how many hours across week, month, or custom ranges.

What it can cover

  • Two-week shift calendar with mobile and desktop views
  • Staff-facing next-shift banner and personal shift list
  • Shift applications and open listing market
  • Direct exchange requests with recipient and admin decision flow
  • Understaffing alerts for managers
  • Staff overview widget for assignments, applications, and exchanges
  • Attendance dashboard with hours, roles, bars, and time slots
  • PWA, offline banner, push notifications, and haptic mobile interactions
