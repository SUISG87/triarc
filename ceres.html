IVMenu & production

Ceres.

From harvest to plate, planned.

The prep list that counts itself.

Ceres is Triarc's menu-planning, recipe, production, and provisioning instrument for professional kitchens: plan once, scale by pax, and turn the result into vendor orders.

Its core is back-of-house math: recipes attach to menus, menus become plans, plans compute production lines and purchase orders grouped by vendor.

How it works

The menu becomes production, then purchasing.

Compose

Ingredients, vendors, recipes, recipe ingredients, menus, and menu recipes hold the kitchen's reusable production knowledge.

Scale

A plan sets the menu, date, service, and pax; Ceres computes cook quantities from recipe yield and ingredient quantities.

Order

Ingredients aggregate across the plan, group by vendor, round toward pack sizes, and become purchase-order lines.

What it can cover

  • Ingredient library with units, vendors, pack sizes, and optional prices
  • Recipe CRUD with yield and recipe-ingredient quantities
  • Menu CRUD with reusable recipe connections
  • Plans by date, service, menu, and pax count
  • Computed production quantities from plan pax and recipe yield
  • Purchase orders grouped by vendor and rounded to pack size
  • Future Zenith sync for order handoff
  • Later guest-facing menu cards once the back-of-house data is reliable
