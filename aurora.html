Aurora.

Next-gen revenue & sales POS.

The cash register, reimagined.

Aurora is Triarc's hospitality POS and revenue instrument: point-of-sale, back-office, stock movement, recipes, dynamic pricing, reports, and guest QR ordering in one professional operating layer.

Built from a hospitality point of view: fast enough for service, precise enough for finance, and calm enough for the end of a long night.

How it works

A till, a back-office, and a live revenue instrument.

Serve

Staff open the POS by PIN, build orders, manage tables or quick sales, split by item or guest, and keep service moving from the iPad.

Settle

Aurora records the transaction, handles card/TWINT/cash-style flows as configured, and keeps failure cases visible for later reconciliation.

Report

The back-office closes sessions, tracks revenue by operational day, connects sales to stock, and prepares finance views for managers.

What it can cover

  • iPad POS for bar staff with PIN-gated venue access
  • Orders, tables, quick pay, comp, split-pax, and split-item flows
  • Inventory and recipe connection so sales can decrement ingredient stock
  • Dynamic market-pricing display and public market surface
  • Admin reporting for revenue, product groups, sessions, and bar-day totals
  • Guest QR ordering as a public ordering layer
  • Worldline/Saferpay payment path with graceful fallback when unavailable
  • Daily close jobs and report emails for operational rhythm
